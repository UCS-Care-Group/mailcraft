**What's new in 0.7.0** (an alpha for testers; sign up in [#11](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/11)): new mail from Microsoft accounts sooner, a proper menu bar on the Mac, and a way to report a problem from inside MailCraft.
- **Mail arrives sooner.** For a Microsoft account, Send/Receive does every Inbox first, shared mailboxes included, skips folders that haven't changed and keeps its connection open. Between runs, MailCraft checks each Inbox every minute ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- **On the Mac**, the menu bar at the top of the screen has MailCraft's menus, and the message list has the thin scroll bar Mac programs use (thanks to @HerbJul and @pick2510).
- **Report a Problem**, in the title bar beside Copy Diagnostics and in Help: say what went wrong, and MailCraft starts the report for you, on GitHub (the problem form filled in, in your browser, with the diagnostics saved to a file to attach; public) or by email (a new message to the maintainers from your own account, with the diagnostics attached; private). You read everything first, and nothing goes until you submit it or press Send.
- **A shared mailbox's calendars.** Each shared mailbox you have opened now has its calendar in the calendar's folder list, under My Calendars, with its address. Tick it and MailCraft asks, once, for your permission to read shared mailboxes' calendars (on Microsoft's page, by itself, never with a sign-in; File › Info shows where it stands, with Ask again), then shows them: the calendars you may read, and as blocks marked Busy, Tentative, Out of Office or Working Elsewhere, with no subject, a calendar its owner shared for free and busy times only. They never raise a reminder. Where you may edit one, you change it from here as you do your own: new items, changes and deletes go to the shared mailbox's own calendar, and a meeting request that arrives in its Inbox is accepted, tentatively accepted, declined or answered with another time, from its address (the first answer asks, once and by itself, for your permission to send from shared mailboxes, with the question its mail asks; until you allow it the answer waits on your computer). A calendar you may only read says so when you try to change it; a change the server refuses is put back and the window says why; and if your rights on a calendar shrink, what you changed and hadn't sent is held and named, not dropped. Removing the shared mailbox removes them from your computer ([#70](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/70)).
- **Sender images** (File › Options, off unless you turn it on): the people you know, your contacts and whoever you have written to, and the senders whose pictures you download (for all mail, a sender or a domain), have their picture in the message list and over the message you read: their Gravatar (told a hash of the address), else their domain's icon from favicon.im or Google (told the domain). Asking tells those services whose pictures you look at, so nobody else's picture is asked for, and none for mail in Junk E-mail; a sender's picture is shown only for mail your mail server says really came from them, so a forged sender keeps their initials. Turning it off stops the asking before the next request ([#363](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/363)).

**Fixes**
- Signing in with a password now works with mail servers that turn away the older LOGIN command: MailCraft uses the server's PLAIN sign-in when it offers one ([#375](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/375)).
- Escape in the search box clears the search and no longer closes a message you are writing ([#365](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/365), thanks to @kartas39).
- In Calendar, an appointment can be moved between your calendar and a shared mailbox's, or one you opened by address, where you may edit both: it is made in the other calendar first and taken out of the old one only once the copy is there. A meeting is not moved to another mailbox's calendar (it would send everyone invited a cancellation); forward it or create it again there ([#70](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/70)).
- In Calendar, Open Shared Calendar (File › Open & Export, Home › Manage Calendars) opens a colleague's calendar by address with your work or school sign-in; it is kept on this computer only, and a sharing invitation has an Open this calendar button ([#70](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/70)).
- In Calendar, calendars that colleagues have shared with you are listed under Shared Calendars, apart from your own, and a calendar you may only read has a lock and says so when you try to add to it ([#70](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/70)).
- "Always download pictures" from a sender or a domain now holds only for mail your mail server says really came from that domain (its `dmarc=pass`), the address is compared as plain ASCII, and mail in a subfolder of Junk E-mail counts as Junk; other mail from a remembered sender asks first, and says why. Your lists are kept.
- A meeting invitation that carries the ID of a meeting already on your calendar, but names another organizer, no longer replaces or removes it; the invitation bar and its question show the organizer's address and say when the message came from another one.
- The question about checking for updates says that Yes also downloads and installs them when you quit.
- MailCraft's log no longer holds subjects, addresses or folder names from Send/Receive problems (they are still in the problems report), and a long sender name no longer hides the address in the reading pane.
- A mailbox opened from a file (File › Open, a double-click, a drop) no longer connects to any server until you have seen where its accounts connect to and pressed Confirm servers: nothing in it is sent, received or run before that. Your own mailbox is not asked about, and neither is a file you confirmed while its accounts stay the same (C1 PR-c).
- A password or sign-in the system remembers for an account is now tied to the mail servers it was given for: an account set to other servers asks again instead of being handed it, and changing an account's server asks again. Entries kept by earlier versions move once, so a downgrade asks once more (C1 PR-b).
- A saved attachment is marked as from the Internet (a zone mark on Windows, quarantine on the Mac), so Office and Gatekeeper treat it with the care they give a download; more kinds that launch when opened (add-in libraries, provisioning packages, connection files) are only saved, and old Office files (.doc, .xls, .ppt and the like) ask before they open; a web link that cannot be read as an address is no longer handed to the browser; an agent given a folder is refused the same kinds of file (batch 5).
- An agent is asked before it deletes a contact, task, event or category, or undoes a change to rules, signatures or accounts; its questions show everything it asked for, and a calendar question names the event and the people invited; no rule moves mail into an Outbox (which would send it); Copy Diagnostics is not available to agents (batch 4).
- A `.mailcraft` file received as an attachment is only saved, never opened from the message; opening another mailbox forgets the passwords and sign-ins held in memory; an agent that asks to add or change an account, or to open a mailbox file, is asked about with the mail servers named (C1 PR-a).
- A calendar invitation or .ics file built to be slow (a time zone with hundreds of parts, thousands of excluded days, or a series counting from 1970) no longer freezes the window: the work a file may cost is bounded, the card still shows, and a note says when a file's time zones were only partly used (PR-d).
- A message with tens of thousands of recipients or categories, a mailbox file of millions of empty messages, an mbox of a very large number of tiny messages or a mailbox file with thousands of accounts and folders no longer exhausts memory or freezes the window: the lists are cut (the reading pane says "and N more"), an import stops at 200,000 messages and says so, and a file with more than the computer can hold is refused (PR-e).
- A mail server that answers one request with an endless stream of mail lists or folders can no longer make MailCraft run out of memory or keep working for hours: an answer is read up to a limit in size, count and time, and past it the request fails and nothing of it is taken in.
- Move to Folder, Copy to Folder and the other folder menus have a search box at the top, so a folder is found by typing part of its name ([#74](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/74), thanks to @strikerz5).
- The message list can be arranged by Categories (View › Arrange By, or the menu at the top of the list). As in Outlook, a message with two categories is listed under both, and still counts once ([#327](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/327), thanks to @kartas39 for suggesting it in [#308](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/308)).
- In a list arranged by Categories, Shift-click and Delete act on the row you clicked when a message is listed under several categories, "No category" is translated, a category named like a heading ("Today", "Pinned") is shown as you wrote it, names that differ only in capitals share one heading, and Find Related counts a message once ([#327](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/327)).
- Show in Groups (View › Arrange By) turns the date headings off for one continuous list, and Single Line (View › Message Preview) shows each message on one line; thanks to @Schintilla for asking ([#214](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/214)).
- Removing an account now removes its mail, attachments and remembered sign-in from this computer, and File › Info › Remove MailCraft's data from this computer removes everything MailCraft keeps here, for an installed or a portable copy; mail on the servers is never touched ([#177](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/177)).
- A shared mailbox, and the next account, no longer wait for your own calendar and contacts to download before their mail starts: calendar and contacts follow once the mail of every account is in, still at start-up and every 15 minutes ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- Ctrl+Q marks the selected messages read and no longer also closes the window ([#269](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/269)).
- Calendar › View shows the four calendar views in place of the messages' controls ([#239](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/239)).
- Add Shared Mailbox no longer waits for a Send/Receive already under way, and once the new mailbox is added the rest of its download no longer holds up your own Send/Receive: your changes go out first, a sign-in left open in the browser no longer makes Add wait, and a Send/Receive you pressed still shows its errors ([#70](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/70), [#257](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/257), [#274](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/274); thanks to @strikerz5 for #257).
- A picture added to a signature that is over the size limit is made smaller instead of refused; when it still can't be taken, the Signatures page says why ([#270](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/270), thanks to @redfoxx15).
- The Appointment window no longer turns the whole program light when Appearance follows a dark system ([#266](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/266)).
- Right-clicking a selected time range in the calendar opens a menu with New Appointment, New Meeting and New All Day Event for that range ([#261](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/261), thanks to @redfoxx15).
- HTML mail laid out with a width, a maximum width, or centring by automatic margins (the usual centred newsletter card) now displays at that width and centred, not full width and misaligned ([#150](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/150)).
- Newsletters, statements and notices keep their padding, margins, lines, rounded corners and line spacing, so their boxes and buttons no longer run into the text ([#71](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/71), thanks to @strikerz5).
- Send/Receive brings new mail in every Microsoft Inbox, shared mailboxes included, first, before the folders and the rest ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- Send/Receive is quicker on a Microsoft account with many folders: your own folders whose message and unread counts haven't changed are skipped, and every folder is still read at the first Send/Receive after opening and once an hour, so flag and category changes there arrive by then ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- A Microsoft account's Send/Receive keeps its connection to Microsoft open between requests instead of opening a new one for each, so it waits less ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- A Microsoft account with many folders lists them in a few requests instead of one for every folder that has subfolders, so Send/Receive gets to the mail sooner ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- New mail in every Microsoft Inbox, shared mailboxes included, shows within a minute: between Send/Receive runs MailCraft quietly checks each Inbox once a minute, as long as Send/Receive runs by itself (File › Options) ([#276](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/276)).
- Arranged by size, each message in the list says its size under its time ([#273](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/273)).
- Send/Receive of a Microsoft account no longer stops part-way through its folders with "InvalidAuthenticationToken" when the sign-in runs out during the run (a long first download, or the computer asleep in the middle of one): the sign-in is renewed once and the run goes on, and one about to run out is renewed before a job starts ([#306](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/306)).
- Send/Receive of a Microsoft account no longer removes a message that was moved into another folder when the list of folders comes back incomplete and misses that folder: the message stays until a Send/Receive with the whole list finds it where it went ([#305](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/305)).
- On a Mac, the message list's rows reach the right edge, with a thin scroll bar over them while scrolling, as in other Mac programs (thanks to @pick2510 for reporting).
- On macOS the menu bar at the top of the screen now has File, Edit, View, Folder, Message, Window and Help as well as MailCraft's own menu, running the same commands with the same ⌘ keys ([#253](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/253), thanks to @HerbJul).
- The settings of an account kept in step through Microsoft Graph no longer show a user name or IMAP and SMTP servers it doesn't use; they say its mail comes through Microsoft Graph instead ([#312](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/312), thanks to @kartas39).
- Messages from Sender, in a message's context menu and Home › Find, finds the sender's mail in every mailbox; a search for a whole address (`from:ada@example.org`) finds that address alone ([#301](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/301)). Inside double quotes in a search, `\"` is now a quote and `\\` a backslash, so a quoted search that ends in a backslash needs it written twice (`subject:"C:\\"`).
- Arranged by size, a message that is only partly here says, and sorts by, its whole size: a Microsoft account's message kept as its preview the size it had when it was downloaded, and a message too large to download from a mail server the size that server gave ([#300](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/300)).
- The only account can now be removed in File › Info; before, it couldn't be ([#313](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/313), thanks to @kartas39).
- Properties… in a folder's or an account's right-click menu shows what it is, where it is and how many messages it holds; its Folder Size… button lists each folder's messages and the space they take ([#314](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/314), thanks to @kartas39).
- A horizontal line in a message (`<hr>`) is drawn again, in the reading pane and above the original in a reply or forward ([#349](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/349)).

**Early alpha.** The foundations are in place and tested, but much of what a daily mail program needs is not there yet, and most of the Microsoft 365 behaviour has only been tested against stand-ins. What works is in the [README](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY#what-works-today).

MailCraft for Windows, macOS, Linux, FreeBSD and the web. Every file is listed with its SHA-256 in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

| Platform | File | |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (x64, x86, ARM64) | `mailcraft-<ver>-windows-<arch>.msi` | Installer for all users: Program Files\MailCraft and the Start menu; offers MailCraft for `mailto:` links and for `.eml`, `.ics` and `.vcf` files without taking them over (choose it in Settings › Default apps); uninstall from Installed apps. `mailcraft-cli.exe` is installed next to the app. |
| | `mailcraft-<ver>-windows-<arch>-portable.zip` | Portable: unzip anywhere and run `mailcraft.exe`; includes `mailcraft-cli.exe`. Registers nothing. |
| macOS 11+ (Apple silicon and Intel) | `mailcraft-<ver>-macos-universal.dmg` | Drag MailCraft to Applications. |
| | `mailcraft-cli-<ver>-macos-universal.zip` | The command-line tool and MCP server alone. |
| Linux x86_64 and aarch64 (glibc 2.35+) | `mailcraft-<ver>-linux-<arch>.AppImage` | `chmod +x` and run (if it does not start, add `--appimage-extract-and-run`). The `.zsync` next to it enables delta updates. |
| | `mailcraft-<ver>-linux-<arch>.deb` / `.rpm` | `sudo apt install ./mailcraft-*.deb` or `sudo dnf install ./mailcraft-*.rpm`. |
| | `mailcraft-<ver>-linux-<arch>.flatpak` | `flatpak install --user mailcraft-*.flatpak` (the freedesktop runtime comes from Flathub). |
| | `mailcraft-<ver>-linux-<arch>.tar.gz` | `bin/mailcraft`, `bin/mailcraft-cli`, the desktop entry, metainfo and icons under `share/`. |
| FreeBSD 15 (x86_64) | `mailcraft-<ver>-freebsd-x86_64.tar.gz` | The two programs, the desktop entry and the icons. |
| Any modern browser | `mailcraft-web-<ver>.zip` | The app as a static site (WebAssembly). It cannot reach mail servers from a browser: bring mail in with File › Import. |

MailCraft keeps your mailbox in its settings folder and saves as you go; one window per mailbox. With a Microsoft 365 or Outlook.com account you sign in on Microsoft's page, and the sign-in is kept by the system's credential store (Windows, macOS, and the Secret Service of GNOME Keyring or KWallet on Linux). What works, and how far each part is from Outlook, is in the [README](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY#what-works-today) and in `docs/gaps.md`.

The builds are not signed with a publisher certificate yet: Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will ask before running them.
