- CalDAV and CardDAV accounts: Add Account finds a server that publishes those services from the domain's mail exchangers, without sending a password, or takes the CalDAV and CardDAV addresses and a sign-in of their own by hand. An account may keep calendars and address books alone, or keep them at another provider than its mail (Nextcloud, say), with its own user name and password; where they are on the mail server and share its sign-in, its password is used. An account of calendars and address books alone may be named by something that isn't an e-mail address. Calendars (shared calendars too) come down over CalDAV and address books (shared books and the organization directory, each contact showing its book) over CardDAV.
