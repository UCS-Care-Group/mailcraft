- **Polish.** MailCraft's window now comes in Polish as a preview language: every text is translated, not yet reviewed line by line by a native speaker, and File › Options names it Polski (wersja zapoznawcza). Dates read day first with a 24-hour clock and weeks from Monday, and counts take Polish's three plural forms ([#1357](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1357)).
