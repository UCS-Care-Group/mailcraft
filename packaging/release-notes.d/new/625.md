- With two accounts or more, the folder pane has **All Accounts** at the top: All Inboxes, All Drafts, All Sent Items, All Deleted Items and All Archives each show that folder of every account in one list, newest first, with the account's colour and name on each message. Whatever you do to a message there (read, flag, move, delete, archive, reply) happens in its own account, just as from that account's own folder. Shared mailboxes are listed with your accounts, and MailCraft opens on All Accounts again if that is where you left it. ([#625](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/625))
