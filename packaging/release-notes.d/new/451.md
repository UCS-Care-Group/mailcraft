- **Moving mail between accounts.** A message you move or copy into another account's folder now goes onto that account's server: from Gmail or another IMAP account into your Microsoft account and back, between two of your own Microsoft accounts, between two IMAP accounts, and from a folder on this computer into any of them. The next Send/Receive of the account it goes to puts it there, with its read state, flag and the time it arrived; the original is removed from its own server only once the copy is there, so a copy that fails never loses it, and the message goes back where it was. A message not fully downloaded yet, or a draft, can't be moved between accounts, and MailCraft says why ([#451](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/451)).
