- **Moving mail between accounts.** A message you move or copy into another account's folder, or a rule files there, now goes onto that account's server: from Gmail or another IMAP account into your Microsoft account and back, between two of your own Microsoft accounts, between two IMAP accounts, and from a folder on this computer into any of them. Send/Receive of the account it goes to puts it there, with its read state, flag and the time it arrived (without who got a blind copy); the original is removed from its own server only once the copy is there, so a copy that fails never loses it, and the message goes back where it was. On an IMAP server, the copy carries one line of MailCraft's own in its header (`X-MailCraft-Place`), seen only in the message's source, by which MailCraft tells it from the same mail that came there by itself. In a Microsoft account, the copy on the server lacks most of the original's headers (such as Received), and the status bar says so. A message from your Microsoft account that isn't downloaded yet moves into an IMAP account at once, and goes onto its server once Send/Receive has downloaded it. A message of which only the headers are here, or a draft, can't go onto another account's server, nor can mail from another mailbox go into a Microsoft account's Outbox, and MailCraft says why (in an IMAP account's Outbox it is held, unsent). An agent asks you first before it moves or copies mail onto another account's server, or makes, turns on or runs a rule that would ([#451](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/451)).
