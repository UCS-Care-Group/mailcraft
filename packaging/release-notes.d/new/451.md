- **Moving mail between accounts.** A message you move or copy into another account's folder now goes onto that account's server: from Gmail or another IMAP account into your Microsoft account and back, between two of your own Microsoft accounts, between two IMAP accounts, and from a folder on this computer into any of them. Send/Receive of the account it goes to puts it there, with its read state, flag and the time it arrived (without who got a blind copy); the original is removed from its own server only once the copy is there, so a copy that fails never loses it, and the message goes back where it was. On an IMAP server, the copy carries one line of MailCraft's own in its header (`X-MailCraft-Place`), seen only in the message's source, by which MailCraft tells it from the same mail that came there by itself. In a Microsoft account, the copy on the server lacks most of the original's headers (such as Received), and the status bar says so. A message not fully downloaded yet, or a draft, can't be moved between accounts, nor can mail from another mailbox go into an Outbox, and MailCraft says why ([#451](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/451)).
