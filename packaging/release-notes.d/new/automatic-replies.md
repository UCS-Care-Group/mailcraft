- File › Info › Automatic Replies, for an account that signs in with Microsoft: turn replies on or off, only during a time range, with one reply for people inside your organisation and another for those outside it (everyone, only your contacts, or nobody). While they go out, the status bar says Automatic Replies. Signing in with Microsoft now asks to read and change your mailbox settings; an account signed in before this can read its automatic replies, and MailCraft offers to sign you in again to change them. Other accounts have none yet: IMAP has no standard way, and Sieve is for later.
