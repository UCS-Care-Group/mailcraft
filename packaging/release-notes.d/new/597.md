- Designed mail keeps its columns and buttons side by side: elements the sender sets as `display: inline-block` (a button, a column with a width) stand on a line, wrap to the next when the line is full, and keep their own width, padding, frame and background; and a picture in a line of text stays in that line and wraps with it instead of jumping out into a block of its own ([#597](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/597)).
