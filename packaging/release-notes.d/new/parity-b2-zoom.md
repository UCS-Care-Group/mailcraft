- **Zoom the message you are reading.** View › Zoom makes the text of the message in the reading pane, or in a window of its own, larger or smaller, from 50% to 300%: Ctrl+Plus and Ctrl+Minus step it (⌘ on a Mac), Ctrl+0 puts it back to 100%, and Ctrl with the mouse wheel over the message does the same. The rest of the window keeps its size (File › Options › Interface size changes that); pictures in the message keep theirs. The zoom is kept between runs (`view.zoom`).
