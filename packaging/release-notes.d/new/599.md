- **Newsletters follow the pane, and their own dark colours.** A newsletter that has one layout for a wide screen and another for a phone (`@media` in its style sheet) now shows the one that fits the reading pane, and changes when you make the pane wider or narrower or switch between the light and dark theme. A newsletter that comes with its own dark colours uses them when MailCraft is dark, and its colours are changed only where they could not be read. Nothing is fetched to decide any of this, but once you allow pictures, one a newsletter shows only for a dark window or a narrow pane is fetched when that look is on screen, so its server can tell from the address that your window is dark and how wide the message is, to within a few pixels. What a sender hid in a message (a line of preview text, the copy meant for the other screen) is still not shown and is not quoted in a reply, but your search and your rules now find it, and an agent you allow to read your mail is given it as part of the message's text (a message already in your mailbox gets this when it is next read from the server) ([#599](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/599)).
