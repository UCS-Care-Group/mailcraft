- Folders within an account, and the Favorites kept in MailCraft, can be put in your own order: drag a folder up or down the folder pane, or use Move Up and Move Down in its right-click menu. Inbox, Drafts, Sent Items and Deleted Items stay at the top, the order is kept on this computer, and Favorites that come from Outlook keep Outlook's order ([#1514](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1514)).
