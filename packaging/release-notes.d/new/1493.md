- The Font menu now offers every font installed on your computer, not only six. The six MailCraft has fallbacks for come first; after them every family your fonts name themselves by, with a field to find one among them and a "Check for new fonts" line for after you install some. What you choose is drawn as its own file draws it, in the composer and in File › Signatures, and goes out as a `font-family` with a font of its kind after it, so a recipient without it gets one of the same kind. Nothing is fetched, installed or shipped with MailCraft; the browser build keeps the six.
