- **Sender warnings.** A message that may not be what it seems says so in a banner above it: red when your mail server says the sender's checks failed, or the sender's name hides another address (another address in the name, or the name of someone you know, or your own, on an address that isn't theirs); amber for an address that looks like one you correspond with but isn't, or a link whose text is a web address and which goes somewhere else (that link is framed, and its tip says so). Mail from outside your organisation has a quiet External tag beside the sender's name, and the message list marks the rows of a failed check or a hidden address. MailCraft makes no checks of its own on the network: what your mail server says, your contacts and Sent Items are what it goes by ([#508](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/508)).
