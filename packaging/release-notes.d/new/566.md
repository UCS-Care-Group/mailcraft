- **Rules kept on a Microsoft account's server.** A rule you make for a Microsoft account is kept on its server, where it runs whether MailCraft is open or not and shows in Outlook on the web and on your phone; the rules made there show in MailCraft too. The Rules window shows one account's rules at a time, the server's apart from this computer's, says where each is kept and why, and has arrows to move a rule up or down and a button that moves a rule from this computer to the server. The first time a rule is to go there, MailCraft asks before Microsoft's page asks for leave to change your mailbox settings; until you allow it, your rules stay on this computer and nothing of them is sent. On a Microsoft account, "From contains" and "Sent to" look in the sender's and recipients' addresses alone, as the server does. A rule the server can't do (flagging, a move to a folder on this computer) stays here and says why. A rule made in Outlook with parts MailCraft doesn't know can be switched on or off, moved or deleted, and is never rewritten ([#566](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/566)).
