- **Rules kept on a Microsoft account's server.** A rule you make for a Microsoft account can be kept on its server, where it runs whether MailCraft is open or not and shows in Outlook on the web and on your phone: Keep on the server is ticked when you start a rule, and you can untick it to keep the rule on this computer. The rules made there show in MailCraft too. The Rules window shows one account's rules at a time, the server's apart from this computer's, says where each is kept and why, and has arrows to move a rule up or down and a button that moves a rule from this computer to the server. The first time a rule is to go there, MailCraft asks before Microsoft's page asks for leave to change your mailbox settings; until you allow it, your rules stay on this computer and nothing of them is sent. On a Microsoft account, "From contains" and "Sent to" look in the sender's and recipients' addresses alone, as the server does. When the server can't do a rule (flagging, a move to a folder of another account), the box is greyed and says why, and the rule stays on this computer. Always Move Messages From This Sender opens the new rule filled in, so you see it and where it is kept before you add it. A rule made in Outlook with parts MailCraft doesn't know can be switched on or off, moved or deleted, and is never rewritten ([#566](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/566)).
