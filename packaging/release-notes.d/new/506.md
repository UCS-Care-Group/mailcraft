- **Merge duplicate contacts.** People › Merge Duplicates finds the contacts that share an e-mail address or a phone number (`+44 20 7946 0000` and `020 7946 0000` are the same number) and goes through them a group at a time: pick the contact to keep and, where their details differ, what it keeps; e-mail addresses, phone numbers, addresses, categories and notes are all kept unless you choose one. Pick several contacts with Ctrl-click (⌘-click on a Mac) to merge just those from the right-click menu. Undo brings the merged contacts back. Contacts from a Microsoft account can't be changed here yet, so they aren't merged, and the dialog says so ([#506](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/506)).
