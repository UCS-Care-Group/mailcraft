- **Exchange server accounts.** Add Account offers Exchange server (your organisation's own), for a mailbox on your organisation's own Exchange Server, Grommunio or SmarterMail. Give your address and password, and MailCraft finds the server, or type its address in Server settings. It signs in with NTLM or with Basic over a secure connection, and if the server's certificate isn't one your system trusts, you see it and decide. This is mail only for now: folders, new mail, flags and moves, drafts and sending. Calendar and contacts come later. A Microsoft 365 mailbox still signs in on Microsoft's page, as a Microsoft account ([#252](https://github.com/UCS-Care-Group/mailcraft/issues/252)).
