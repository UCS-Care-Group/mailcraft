- Each account can now have its own font. File › Signatures has a **Default fonts** section under the default signatures: one row per account — a shared mailbox of its own — with a button for each of its three fonts: what new messages are written in, what replies and forwards are written in, and what plain text is written and read in. A button opens the Font dialog — a family, a style, a size, a colour, underline, and a sample drawn in what you've chosen so far — with the same family list the composer's Font menu has: the six MailCraft has fallbacks for and every family your fonts name themselves by. What you choose is what that account's mail is written in from then on; a signature and the message you're answering keep their own looks, and leaving one alone leaves the message's own font standing.
