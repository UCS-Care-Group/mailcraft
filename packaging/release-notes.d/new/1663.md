- Select several appointments in the calendar — click, Ctrl+click, Shift+click or Ctrl+A — and copy, cut, paste or delete them together, from the keys, an item's menu or the Home tab's Clipboard group. A move keeps an ordinary appointment's id, UID and details, its length and its spacing; a day of a series is changed as that day, the series never cancelled and made again; the whole set is one Undo; a cut stays where it is until it is pasted, and nothing changes if any item cannot go. The calendar keeps its own clipboard, and the system clipboard only receives a short text that says what was copied or cut. When deleting or moving the set would make an account's server write to people, you are asked once first, and the question lists the appointments under each account whose server sends the mail, each with its day and time (for a move, its new time too) and the people it goes to; somebody else's meetings under a heading that names the organizers a decline would tell; and what writes to nobody under a heading of its own: every appointment is listed, the first twelve that write to people with up to six names each and the rest with how many. Somebody invited to two of them is counted once, and somebody invited without an address is counted too. Copy, Cut and Paste also work from command search ([#1663](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1663)).
