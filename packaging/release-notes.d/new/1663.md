- Select several appointments in the calendar — click, Ctrl+click, Shift+click or Ctrl+A — and copy, cut, paste or delete them together, from the keys, an item's menu or the Home tab's Clipboard group. A move keeps an ordinary appointment's id, UID and details, its length and its spacing; a day of a series is changed as that day, the series never cancelled and made again; the whole set is one Undo; a cut stays where it is until it is pasted, and nothing changes if any item cannot go. The calendar keeps its own clipboard, and the system clipboard only receives a short text that says what was copied or cut ([#1663](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1663)).
