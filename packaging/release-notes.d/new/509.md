- **Warnings before sending.** MailCraft asks before a message goes to an address that looks like someone you write to but isn't (a domain, or the part before the @, one character away from a contact's or from someone you've sent mail to), and before 5 or more people outside your organisation, at 2 or more domains, would see each other's addresses in To and Cc (with personal accounts only, there's no organisation, and this isn't asked). You can send anyway, cancel, or, for the outsiders, move them to Bcc in one click. A Reply All to people who were already on the message together isn't asked about. When an agent sends, you're asked in the window and the agent can't answer for you; a script without the window is refused unless it says to send anyway ([#509](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/509)).
