- **Rules can be changed once they are made.** Edit Rule, in a rule's menu in the Rules window, opens the rule on its page with Save and Cancel. A rule kept on a Microsoft account's server is changed there at the next Send/Receive; a rule made in Outlook with parts MailCraft can't show is still changed in Outlook. A rule from before a rule was for one account, under All accounts, is given to one account with For One Account in its menu, and is then changed as any; one that moves or copies mail to another account's folder opens on its page for the folder to be chosen again, and stays under All accounts until it is saved ([#566](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/566)).
