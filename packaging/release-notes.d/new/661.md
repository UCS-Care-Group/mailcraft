- **Categories kept in step with Outlook.** On a Microsoft account (not one still on IMAP), a category you make, delete, rename or give another colour in MailCraft now changes in Outlook too, a few seconds later, and what you change in Outlook shows in MailCraft at the next sync; before, a category you deleted or renamed here came back. A Microsoft account's categories take Outlook's 25 colours. Keeping your tasks, rules, automatic replies and categories in step with Outlook (on a Microsoft account still on IMAP, its tasks and automatic replies) needs your permission to change your To Do lists and your mailbox settings: a new account is asked on Microsoft's sign-in page, and an account you added before is asked once, in one question (Keep in Step with Outlook) with one Microsoft page for all of it, in place of the separate questions there were. File › Info has one line for it per account, with Ask again. Until you allow it, that account's categories can't be changed in MailCraft, and the Categories window says so, with Allow. A category that was only on this computer stays there and says so, with Add to Outlook ([#661](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/661)).
