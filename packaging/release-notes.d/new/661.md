- **Categories kept in step with Outlook.** On a Microsoft account (not one still on IMAP), a category you make, delete, rename or give another colour in MailCraft now changes in Outlook too, a few seconds later, and what you change in Outlook shows in MailCraft within a few minutes; before, a category you deleted or renamed here came back. A rename gives your mail, events and to-dos the new name in Outlook too, and the old name leaves Outlook's list once they have it; mail older than what MailCraft keeps offline, events outside the days the calendar keeps here or in a calendar you may only read, and contacts keep the old name there. A Microsoft account's categories take Outlook's 25 colours. Keeping your tasks, rules, automatic replies and categories in step with Outlook (on a Microsoft account still on IMAP, its tasks and automatic replies) needs your permission to change your To Do lists and your mailbox settings: a new account is asked on Microsoft's sign-in page, and an account you added before is asked in one question (Keep in Step with Outlook) with one Microsoft page for all of it, in place of the separate questions there were. If you close the question, it isn't put again until MailCraft starts again or you open another mailbox; File › Info has one line for it per account, with Ask again. Until you allow it, and until MailCraft has checked (it can't while offline), that account's categories can't be changed in MailCraft, nor a new one put on its items, and the Categories window says so, with Allow. A category that was only on this computer stays there and says so, with Add to Outlook ([#661](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/661)).
