- Outgoing mail can be Encrypted: Options › Encrypt in a new message, or "Encrypt when every recipient has a key" for an account, with S/MIME or OpenPGP keys kept as Public keys. The window says before Send whether it will be encrypted; a recipient without a key stops Send and offers Send unencrypted, Remove those recipients or Cancel, and a message that can't be encrypted is held in the Outbox, never sent in the clear. Mail you Encrypt is also encrypted to your own key, so Sent Items can be read; To and Cc get one message and each Bcc recipient a message of their own, so nobody learns who was blind-copied; Sent Items keeps the Encrypted message, never the text; a draft with Encrypt on is kept Encrypted to your own key, goes up to a Microsoft or Exchange server account as the encrypted message alone, and can't be opened to edit until decrypting is built ([#30](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/30)).
