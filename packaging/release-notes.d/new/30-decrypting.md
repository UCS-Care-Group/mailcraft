- Encrypted mail you receive is now opened where you read it. An S/MIME message opens with the certificate in your system's store or on your smartcard, and an OpenPGP message (PGP/MIME or in the text) with the key GnuPG's agent holds; a smartcard's PIN is asked in the window. What it says is held in memory only: never saved in your mailbox, never in a search, a rule, a preview or a notification, and never given to an agent. A bar says it was opened, or why it wasn't, with Try Again, and a Digital signature inside is checked too. Nothing is encrypted when you send yet ([#30](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/30)).
