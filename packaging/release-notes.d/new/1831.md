- Sign in with your own identity provider: an IMAP account on your own mail server (Dovecot and Postfix, say) can sign in on your Keycloak, Authentik or other OpenID Connect page instead of with a password. Press Discover to find its addresses from its own, or type them; the mail servers get the token with OAUTHBEARER or XOAUTH2. Account Settings now shows every value a Microsoft, Google or own-provider sign-in uses. See [Signing in with your own identity provider](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/blob/main/docs/oauth-servers.md) ([#1831](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1831)).
