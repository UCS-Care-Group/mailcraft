- Exchange server accounts keep their calendars, address books and to-do lists in step, both ways, after the mail of a Send/Receive: appointments and meetings (including the people invited, the place, the notes and the reminder), contacts (with their addresses, phone numbers, notes and birthday) and to-dos (with their dates, importance and completion). Another person's calendar or address book can be opened by address when they have shared it, and is read with the owner's sign-in; it is listed under Shared Calendars or with the account's books. Tried against a Grommunio server; Exchange Server itself has been tried for mail and reading only.
