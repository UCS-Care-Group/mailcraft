- **Messages on a light background in the dark theme.** File › Options › General has a new choice under the interface theme: *Show messages on a light background in the dark theme*. With it on, a message is shown as its sender made it, in its own colours on a white page, in the reading pane and in a message's own window, while the rest of the window stays dark; a newsletter that comes with its own dark colours shows its light ones there. It is off at first, and changes nothing in the light theme ([#634](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/634)).
