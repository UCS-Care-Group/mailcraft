- **Write more than one message at once.** New Email, Reply, Reply All, Forward and opening a draft no longer ask you to save or discard the message you are writing: they open the new one, and the one you were writing stays open as it was. When you send or close the new one, the one before it comes back. Quitting, or opening another mailbox, asks once about every message with changes. Windows of their own for messages being written come next ([#286](https://github.com/UCS-Care-Group/mailcraft/issues/286)).
