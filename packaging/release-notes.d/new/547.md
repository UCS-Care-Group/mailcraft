- **Pictures in the message you write.** Insert › Pictures puts a picture in the text, and Ctrl+V pastes a picture you copied (a screenshot, say) when nothing else is on the clipboard. It sits in the line like a letter: the caret goes over it, Backspace and Delete remove it, Undo brings it back. It goes out as part of the message (SMTP and Microsoft accounts alike), stays in drafts, and a plain-text copy says `[picture]`. Pictures are PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 256 KB each, 512 KB and ten per message, as in signatures ([#547](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/547)).
