- **Safe Senders and Blocked Senders.** Home › Delete › Junk now has Block Sender, Never Block Sender, Never Block Sender's Domain and Junk E-mail Options, where both lists can be added to and taken from, an address or a whole domain at a time. New mail from a blocked sender goes to Junk E-mail as it arrives, and a safe sender's mail that arrives in Junk E-mail goes to the Inbox, moved on the server as your own moves are. The lists are kept on this computer with your mailbox, and a domain means that domain only, not its subdomains ([#504](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/504)).
