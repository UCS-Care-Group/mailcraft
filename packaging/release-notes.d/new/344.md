- **The message list in columns.** As in Outlook: turn them on in View › Message Preview › Columns. Click a heading to sort by it, and again to reverse; drag its edge to make the column wider or narrower. When the list is too narrow for all its columns, beside the reading pane for one, it shows its usual rows until it has room ([#344](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/344)).
