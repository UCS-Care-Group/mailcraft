- **The message list in columns.** As in Outlook: turn them on in View › Message Preview, and choose them with View › Add Columns. Drag a heading to move its column, or off the row to remove it. Click a heading to sort by it, and again to reverse. When the list is too narrow for all its columns, beside the reading pane for one, it shows its usual rows until it has room ([#344](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/344)).
