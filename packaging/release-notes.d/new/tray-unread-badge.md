- With Close to system tray enabled, MailCraft’s icon now shows a little red circle at the top left while an Inbox of your own server accounts has unread mail. Reading or removing the last unread Inbox message clears it, even when desktop alerts are turned off; shared mailboxes, local and imported mail, Archive and custom folders do not count. Checked on Fedora 45 and macOS; Windows is still to be checked ([#544](https://github.com/UCS-Care-Group/mailcraft/issues/544)).
