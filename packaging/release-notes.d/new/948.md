- **Follow-up reminders.** Follow Up › Add Reminder… flags a message with a date and time; the reminders window raises it then (Dismiss, Snooze, a double click opens the message), once, also if MailCraft was closed at the time. For mail you sent, and in the message you are writing (Follow Up), "Remind me if nobody replies" after 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days or on a date: if nobody the message went to has written back in its conversation by then, the reminder is raised and the message flagged; a reply that arrives first cancels it. Clearing the flag or marking the message complete ends the reminder. The reminder time is kept on this computer only; the flag syncs with the account as before ([#948](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/948)).
