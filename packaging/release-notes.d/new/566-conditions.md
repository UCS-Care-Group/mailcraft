- **Rules: more of Outlook's conditions, exceptions and actions.** A rule can now look for mail from or sent to particular people, sent only to you, with your address in To or Cc, words in the subject or body or a header, a sensitivity, a size, a meeting invitation, an automatic reply, a category, a flag or the days it came, with several words or addresses in one, set apart by semicolons. "Sent to" is now the condition for particular people; the one that looks for words in the recipients is called "Recipient contains". Each of them can also be an exception (Except if), and a rule can copy mail to a folder, delete it for good, flag it with a due day, clear its flag or its categories, and show a desktop alert for the mail it acts on as it arrives, even when alerts for new mail are off (as those, not while the MailCraft window is in front). On a Microsoft account, a rule the server can do is kept there; one that deletes for good, looks at the days mail came, flags mail, clears a flag or categories, copies mail out of the mailbox, copies it to more than one folder or to one not on the server yet, has two exceptions of one kind that look for different things and not for words (two importances, say), or shows an alert stays on this computer. On a Microsoft or Exchange server account, a rule on this computer that looks at the text of new mail, a header, its size, its sensitivity or whether it is a meeting invitation or an automatic reply, or that copies it into another account's folder, acts once the message is downloaded whole (a message too large or too old to download by itself, when you open it), and shows its desktop alert then; a later rule that deletes for good waits with it ([#566](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/566)).
