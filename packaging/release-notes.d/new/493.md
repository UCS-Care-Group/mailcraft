- **Search Subfolders and Current Mailbox.** The menu at the right of the search box now offers four places to look, as classic Outlook does: Current Folder, Subfolders (the folder in view and every folder under it), Current Mailbox (every folder of the account in view, a shared mailbox counting as one of its own) and All Mailboxes. Current Mailbox and All Mailboxes leave out Deleted Items and Junk E-mail. Each result of a search of more than one folder shows its folder under its time, and More results on the server asks only the account in view ([#493](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/493)).
