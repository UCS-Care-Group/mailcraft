- Unsubscribe from a mailing list from the bar above one of its messages, or its right-click menu: in one click where the list allows it (RFC 8058), by a message from your account, or on the list's own page in your browser. MailCraft asks first and says what is sent and to whom, and offers it only for mail your mail server confirms came from the list, outside Junk E-mail and with no warning about its sender ([#1207](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1207)).
