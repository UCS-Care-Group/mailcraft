- Automatic sentence capitals in the composer use the writing language’s case rules and can be switched off in File › Options › Mail. Undo restores the original case. Pasted text, addresses, mixed-case names, common abbreviations and modern Georgian text keep their case. Ordinal dates keep their lowercase month in Czech, Danish, Finnish, German, Hungarian, Norwegian, Polish and Slovak ([#1717](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1717)).
