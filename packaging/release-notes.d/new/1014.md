- **JMAP accounts in the desktop app.** An account on a JMAP server (RFC 8620/8621) is now read in the desktop app as in the browser build: its mailboxes, the newest messages and their marks come down, and messages are fetched whole when opened. Changes still don't go up and nothing is sent from such an account yet; see docs/jmap.md ([#1014](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1014)).
