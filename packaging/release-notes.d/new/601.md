- **Pictures behind the text of designed mail.** A newsletter or a piece of stationery that puts a picture behind a table, a cell or the whole message (a banner photograph with a headline on it, a paper texture, a gradient) now shows it, at the size and place it asks for, once or repeated. A picture that is attached to the message or written into it is shown straight away; one on the web is downloaded only when you choose Download pictures (or always download pictures from that sender), like any other picture. Its text is made readable against the colour the sender gave the box, as before; in the dark theme the picture is dimmed so the text stays readable, and with high contrast on it is left out ([#601](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/601)).
