- **Pictures behind the text of designed mail.** A newsletter or a piece of stationery that puts a picture behind a table, a cell or the whole message (a banner photograph with a headline on it, a paper texture, a gradient) now shows it, at the size and place it asks for, once or repeated. A picture that is attached to the message or written into it is shown straight away; one on the web is downloaded only when you choose Download pictures (or always download pictures from that sender), like any other picture. Its text is made readable against the colour the sender gave the box, as before (where the sender gave none, the picture is softened so its text stays readable); in the dark theme the picture is dimmed, and with high contrast on it is left out. Nothing hidden in the message, and no picture behind a box of a pixel or two, is ever downloaded ([#601](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/601)).
