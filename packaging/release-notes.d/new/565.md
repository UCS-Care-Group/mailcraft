- **SVG pictures in mail.** A picture in a message that is a simple SVG drawing (as logos and icons usually are) used to be a broken picture; it is now shown, sharp at any size, whether it is attached to the message, written into it or, once you let a message's pictures come, on the web. A drawing is shown as a picture and nothing more: nothing it refers to is ever downloaded or read from your computer, no font is looked for, so words in a drawing are left out, and nothing in it runs. A drawing that is damaged, large or elaborate (with style sheets, repeated parts, patterns, masks or filters, among others) is still shown as a broken picture ([#565](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/565)).
