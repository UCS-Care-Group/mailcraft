- **SVG pictures in mail.** A picture in a message that is a flat SVG drawing (as many logos and icons are: shapes and colour gradients) used to be a broken picture; it is now shown, sharp at any size, whether it is attached to the message, written into it or, once you let a message's pictures come, on the web. A reply or forward still carries only PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP pictures, never a drawing. A drawing is shown as a picture and nothing more: nothing it refers to is ever downloaded or read from your computer, and nothing in it runs. A drawing that is damaged, large or more elaborate than that (with words, clipping, masks, filters, repeated parts or style sheets, among others) is still shown as a broken picture, as is every drawing in the browser version of MailCraft ([#565](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/565)).
