- [#1373](https://github.com/UCS-Care-Group/mailcraft/issues/1373): **Start MailCraft when you sign in.** File › Options › General has Start MailCraft when you sign in, off by default. MailCraft then adds itself to your computer's own list of programs started at sign-in (the autostart folder on Linux and the BSDs, Startup apps on Windows, a launch agent on macOS) and opens minimized at sign-in, or straight into the system tray when Close to system tray is on. Removing it there turns the switch off here too. Not offered in the Flatpak.
