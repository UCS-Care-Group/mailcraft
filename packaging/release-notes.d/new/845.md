- **The calendar as a list.** Home › Arrange › List (Ctrl+Alt+5) shows the appointments of the calendars you show as a table, a series as one row: click a heading to sort by it and again to reverse, and choose the groups in View › Arrange By (by how they repeat, by category, or none), as in Outlook's List view ([#845](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/845)).
