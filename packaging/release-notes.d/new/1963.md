- On the Android app, a message open on the phone moves to the next message with a swipe left and to the one above with a swipe right, in the list you opened it from (folder, filter, search, sort; by conversation in conversation view). Wide tables and pictures scroll first, a swipe from the screen's edge stays Back, the ends of the list give a small bounce, and TalkBack has Next message and Previous message actions. A message swiped to is marked read after a moment, so passing by quickly leaves it unread. ([#1963](https://github.com/UCSLabs/mailcraft/issues/1963))
