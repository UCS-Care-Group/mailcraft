- **Open a conversation in the message list.** With View › Show as Conversations on, a conversation's row has an arrow: click it or press Right to list its messages under the row, newest first, and click it again or press Left to close it. A reply you sent is listed there too, with "Sent Items" beside it, so the row's count is the whole conversation's, as in the reading pane. A message under the row can be read, moved or deleted on its own. Delete on a conversation's row deletes its messages in that folder and leaves your replies in Sent Items; before it deletes more than one message it asks, with Don't ask again for this account, and Account Settings › Deleting turns the question back on ([#503](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/503)).
