- Accent colour ([#1737](https://github.com/UCS-Care-Group/mailcraft/issues/1737)): File › Options › General › Accent has a fifth swatch, **Custom color**, which opens a colour picker with a hex field, so the window's accent can be any colour of your own. The colour is fitted to the theme it sits in — darkened on a light window, lightened on a dark one — so text and outlines keep their contrast in the light and the dark theme and in high contrast. Kept in `ui.json` as `customAccent`; `ui.accent` takes `{"accent": "custom", "color": "#rrggbb"}`, or `{"accent": "#rrggbb"}`.
