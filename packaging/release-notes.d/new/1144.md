- **Move to Contacts Folder…** puts a contact, or several picked together, in another contacts folder: from this computer into one of your accounts (so it is on your other devices too), from one account or folder to another, or back onto this computer. It is in the People ribbon, the contact's right-click menu and its card, and in the person's menu on Android. When the folder has somebody with the same e-mail address already, you can open that one instead or move anyway. The contact is removed from where it was only once the new folder's server has it, so nothing is lost if a step fails, and Undo takes the whole move back. A contact still on its way to its server waits before it can go into another account's or folder's server (and, once a try has failed, before it can go back onto this computer): it is named, and the rest of a selection moves ([#1144](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1144)).
