- Outgoing mail can be Digitally signed: Options › Sign in a new message, or Always for an account under its Digital Signatures, with an S/MIME certificate from the system's certificate store, keychain or a smartcard, or an OpenPGP key in GnuPG (a smartcard too). A message that can't be signed is held in the Outbox and never sent unsigned ([#30](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/30)).
