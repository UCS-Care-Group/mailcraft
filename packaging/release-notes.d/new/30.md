- OpenPGP Digital signatures are now checked in the reading pane; Public keys can be imported, listed and removed ([#30](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/30)).
- File › Options › Security lists your own certificates and keys and imports a `.p12` or an OpenPGP secret key; the Public keys kept for other people are listed and removed there, an OpenPGP key is marked as verified after comparing its fingerprint, and a contact's card shows its certificates ([#30](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/30)).
- Outgoing mail can be Digitally signed: Options › Sign in a new message, or Always for an account under its Digital Signatures, with an S/MIME certificate from the system's certificate store, keychain or a smartcard, or an OpenPGP key in GnuPG (a smartcard too). A message that can't be signed is held in the Outbox and never sent unsigned ([#30](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/30)).
