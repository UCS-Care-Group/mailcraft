- **Classic Light theme.** File › Options › General › Interface theme offers Classic Light: one flat white page with square corners, the ribbon's tabs in capitals with File in your accent colour, the status bar in your accent colour, a bar marking the selected message, and the ribbon and message list closer together, for people used to the desktop mail programs of the early 2010s. Your accent colour and high contrast work with it ([#1203](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1203)).
