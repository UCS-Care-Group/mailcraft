- Messages can be selected from the keyboard: in the message list, Shift+Down and Shift+Up add the next or the previous message to the selection, and going back the other way takes them out again; Home and End go to the first and the last message, and Page Up and Page Down a page at a time, each with Shift to select as far as it goes. A held key moves a message at a time, and the list scrolls to keep up. From scripts and agents: `mail.next` and `mail.previous` with `extend`, and `mail.first`, `mail.last`, `mail.pageDown` and `mail.pageUp` ([#426](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/426), thanks to @kartas39).
