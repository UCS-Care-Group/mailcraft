- **Sign in with Google on the desktop.** New Gmail and Google Workspace accounts offer Google sign-in first when MailCraft's Desktop registration is configured, with **Use an app password instead** as a separate alternative. Existing accounts are not changed automatically: **Switch to Google sign-in** requires successful sign-in to the same primary Google address (Workspace aliases are not supported as OAuth account identities), and canceled conversion leaves the old credential in use. Conversion and **Reconnect with Google** keep downloaded messages and folders. Access tokens stay in memory; remembered refresh grants use the operating system's credential store. A build without a registration explains the limitation and retains app-password setup. The official registration and applicable public Google verification remain release requirements; browser Gmail is separate. See the [account guide](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/blob/main/docs/google-accounts.md) ([#232](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/232)).
