- Spelling (#954): in the desktop composer on Windows and macOS, installed system dictionaries mark misspellings as you write. Right-click a marked word for suggestions, or choose a dictionary and find the next error under Review › Spelling. The check stays on your computer.
- Automatic capitals: MailCraft capitalizes the first word of a sentence when you finish typing it. Undo restores the original case. Turn the feature off in File › Options › Mail.
