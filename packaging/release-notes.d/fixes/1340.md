- Opening and changing many messages no longer keeps their bodies and attachments in memory through the steps Undo can take back, for Microsoft, Exchange and JMAP accounts too: what the memory budget lets go of, the Undo and Redo steps let go of too, and Undo reads it from disk again when it is wanted ([#1340](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1340)).
- MailCraft keeps less in memory for showing things again quickly: at most 96 MB of picture textures (was 160 MB), 48 MB and 150 of the pictures downloaded from the web (was 96 MB and 300), 300 senders' pictures (was 1,000) and 64 MB of content read from disk (was 128 MB). What is on screen is always kept ([#1340](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1340)).
- MailCraft lets go of memory once its window has been minimised, or wholly covered, for three minutes: the pictures, the laid-out messages, the message fonts and the bodies and attachments read from disk (except those of messages open in a window, selected or being written) go, and come back from disk when they are shown again. Nothing goes while a Send/Receive, a save, an import or a sending is under way ([#1340](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1340)).
