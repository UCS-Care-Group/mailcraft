- A repeating meeting in a time zone whose clocks jump over midnight (for example America/Nuuk on the last Saturday of March, or Santiago and Havana on the night summer time begins), with the occurrence on that day skipped, no longer comes back with the following day's occurrence skipped instead when it is exported or sent as an invitation and read again. A time in such a jump in an invitation that defines its own time zone is no longer reported as a time zone defined otherwise than its name.
