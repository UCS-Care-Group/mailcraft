- When you go to another tab or search, nothing stays selected, so Delete and Move take only the messages you can see. A message you open from Tasks, People or a new-mail alert opens in its folder's All tab. Before, a message selected in one tab stayed selected in the next, where the list didn't show it, and Ctrl+click and Delete removed it too ([#485](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/485)).
