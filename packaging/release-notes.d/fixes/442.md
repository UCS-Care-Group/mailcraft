- Escape, the ✕ on the message's bar, New Email, Reply, Reply All, Forward, Forward as Attachment and Open Draft no longer throw away the message you are writing without a word: when you have typed or added something in it, they ask Unsaved Message first, as quitting does (Save Draft, Discard or Cancel), and a draft that can't be saved leaves the message open and says why. When an agent or `mailcraft-cli` does one of these, nobody is asked: the message is saved to Drafts first ([#442](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/442), thanks to @kartas39).
