- HTML mail looks more as it does in Outlook: links are underlined only where the message doesn't say otherwise, so a shop's or a service's mail no longer has a line under every button, heading and price; buttons are drawn as buttons, as wide as their card where they ask to be, with their words in the middle, instead of as highlighted words; labels and badges are rounded boxes; mail from templates that nest their layout deeply (a shop's delivery notice) keeps its buttons and boxes; a button whose border is its own colour has no darker edge; and a message whose plain-text copy lost a character no longer says that characters couldn't be read when that copy isn't what is shown ([#842](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/842)).
