- A file dragged from the desktop onto a New Email, reply or forward in a window of its own is attached, instead of nothing happening. A picture dropped on a message in HTML goes into its text, at the caret, made smaller to fit as Insert › Pictures makes it (at most 1920 pixels wide and 5 MB); to send a photo as it is, attach it with Attach File. A picture the text can't take (an animated GIF over 5 MB, or more than 30 pictures or 20 MB of them in one message) is attached instead, as is any picture dropped on a plain-text message or while a signature is being edited. Dropped on an HTML message being written in the reading pane, a picture now goes into the text too, rather than being attached ([#1280](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1280), [#1742](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1742)).
