- A file dragged from the desktop onto a New Email, reply or forward in a window of its own is attached, instead of nothing happening. A picture dropped on a message in HTML goes into its text, at the caret; in a plain-text message, or while a signature is being edited, it is attached instead. Dropped on an HTML message being written in the reading pane, a picture now goes into the text too, rather than being attached ([#1280](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1280), [#1742](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1742)).
