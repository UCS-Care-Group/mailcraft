- HTML mail shows side by side the tables a sender sets at the left and at the right of a line, as browsers and Outlook do: a bill's label at the left with its value at the right, a greeting beside a notice. Each stood on a line of its own. A box framed with the lines of table cells keeps its heading inside the frame; it stood outside it. And boxes laid out with `display: inline-block` that are tables stand in one line, as other boxes do.
