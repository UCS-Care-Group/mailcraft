- With a message being written in the reading pane (a draft after Continue editing, a New Email, a reply), clicking a message in the list now saves what you were writing to Drafts and shows the message you clicked, so that Delete, Reply and the rest act on the message you see, never on one hidden behind the message being written; Next Message, Select All and a new-mail alert do the same, and the list's keys no longer move a selection you can't see. A draft with no changes isn't saved again, a message in a window of its own stays open, and a draft that can't be saved stays in the reading pane with the selection as it was ([#727](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/727)).
