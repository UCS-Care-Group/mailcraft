- On Wayland, Ctrl+V and a text field's Paste menu use one clipboard reader with a two-second timeout and a 64 MiB text limit, including in messages and signatures. A competing reader no longer takes its selection, and text can be copied without X11. Picture-only selections still reach picture paste; failed or oversized transfers are not retried, and menu paste stays with its original field ([#1217](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1217)).
