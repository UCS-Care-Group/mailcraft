- Escape and Enter now go to one place: a dialog or question that is open, else the window you last clicked or typed in, as with the windows of your system ([#406](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/406)). Escape with a drop-down list or a menu open in a dialog (such as the phone type in New Contact), or in an appointment's recurrence editor, closes only the list or the editor; before, the whole dialog closed and what was typed in it was lost. Not yet the list of people suggested under To, Cc, Bcc or an attendee line: Escape there still goes to the message or meeting it is in, as before. Holding Escape closes one message window, not two, and no longer answers the "save changes?" question before you have read it. The repeats of a held Escape or Enter that closes a dialog no longer open the contact, task or appointment under it, end a search, or drop a picked time in the calendar. Escape closes Send/Receive Progress before a message window under it.
- Holding Ctrl+W in a message or appointment window now closes only that window, and holding Ctrl+Enter or Alt+S saves or sends only the appointment or meeting in front. Before, the key's repeats went on to the windows behind it ([#406](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/406)).
- While a message box was open over an appointment window, what you typed went into the appointment under it, and Enter both closed the message box and saved the appointment. Now the message box has the keyboard until it closes, and then the field you were in has it again ([#406](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/406)).
- Holding Enter on a selected contact or task no longer opens it and then, with the key's repeats, presses its OK and closes it again ([#406](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/406)).
- With an appointment window open over the main window, Enter after a click on a message, contact, task or appointment in the main window no longer saves and closes the appointment as well ([#406](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/406)).
