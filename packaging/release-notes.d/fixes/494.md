- An agent no longer takes the window off what you are writing or changing without asking: while you are writing a message you have typed in, saved or not, an agent that wants to start another, reply, forward, open a draft, close it or open another mailbox file waits for your Allow (what isn't saved goes to Drafts first), and one that wants to throw your text away or discard the message waits for your Delete; an appointment, a contact or a task with unsaved changes, in a dialog or a window of its own, is closed by an agent only on your Delete, and an agent can't put another dialog in its place. No permission given to the agent in advance skips these questions. People › Email while you are writing now asks Save Draft, Discard or Cancel first, as New Email does ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
