- An agent no longer closes the message you are writing, or opens another in its place, without asking you, whatever you allowed it in advance; what isn't saved goes to Drafts first ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
- An agent no longer throws away what you typed in a message without your Delete, and can't put back an earlier saved version of it with an undo ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
- An agent no longer closes, deletes or replaces an appointment, a contact or a task with changes you haven't saved, in the dialog or in an appointment's own window, without asking you, and can't change the time or the recurrence you are filling in ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
- People › Email while you are writing a message now asks Save Draft, Discard or Cancel first, as New Email does, instead of saving the message to Drafts without asking ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
- Closing a message, or quitting, when its saved draft was deleted meanwhile now asks Save Draft, Discard or Cancel, instead of closing it and losing what you wrote ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
