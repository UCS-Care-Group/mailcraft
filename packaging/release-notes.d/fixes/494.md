- An agent no longer takes the window off what you are writing or changing without asking: while a message has changes of yours that aren't saved, an agent that wants to start another, reply, forward, open a draft or close it waits for your Allow (what isn't saved goes to Drafts first), and one that wants to throw your text away waits for your Delete; an appointment, a contact or a task with unsaved changes is closed by an agent only on your Delete. No permission given to the agent in advance skips these questions. People › Email while you are writing now asks Save Draft, Discard or Cancel first, as New Email does ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
