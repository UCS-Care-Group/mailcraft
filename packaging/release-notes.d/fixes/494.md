- An agent no longer takes the window off what you are writing or changing without asking: while you are writing a message you have typed in, saved or not, an agent that wants to start another, reply, forward, open a draft or close it waits for your Allow (what isn't saved goes to Drafts first), one that wants to open another mailbox file, which closes the message without saving it, asks too, and one that wants to throw your text away or discard the message waits for your Delete; an appointment, a contact or a task with unsaved changes, in a dialog or a window of its own, is closed by an agent only on your Delete, also when the agent would delete the appointment in a window itself; an agent can't take such an item away any other way (declining the meeting, deleting the contact or the task you are changing in the dialog, removing a meeting its organizer called off), change the time or the recurrence you are filling in, or close the window with that recurrence, nor put another dialog in its place. No permission given to the agent in advance skips these questions; an agent you let click and type in the window does there what you could. Closing a message, or quitting, when its saved draft was deleted meanwhile asks Save Draft, Discard or Cancel, as for unsaved changes. People › Email while you are writing now asks Save Draft, Discard or Cancel first, as New Email does ([#494](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/494)).
