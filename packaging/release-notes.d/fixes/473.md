- An agent without the Delete permission can no longer remove mail through Undo or Redo (undoing a copy or an import, redoing a permanent delete): such a step now asks for Delete, or is refused before anything moves. A step that also puts back the accounts, or a change that writes to people, is asked about once, the question names the messages, drafts or contacts it takes away, and the owner's yes covers both ([#473](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/473)).
