- HTML mail looks more as it does in Outlook: links are underlined only where the message doesn't say otherwise, so a shop's or a service's mail no longer has a line under every button, heading and price; buttons are drawn as buttons, as wide as their card where they ask to be, with their words in the middle, instead of as highlighted words; buttons and columns that a template writes side by side (a bill's two buttons, a logo beside a title) stand side by side instead of one under the other; a newsletter's heading is at the left of its column instead of in the middle; labels and badges are rounded boxes; mail from templates that nest their layout deeply (a shop's delivery notice) keeps its buttons and boxes; a button whose border is its own colour has no darker edge; and a message whose plain-text copy lost a character no longer says that characters couldn't be read when that copy isn't what is shown ([#774](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/pull/774)).
