- In People, the search box now always shows the search that filters the contacts. Before, a click outside the box lost the letters, while the list stayed filtered by them. Ctrl+1 and Ctrl+3 while you type in the search box now show the search of the place you go to; before, the old letters stayed in the box and went into the other search. And Ctrl+E puts the cursor at the end of the search in the box; before, what you typed could land in the middle of it after an agent changed the search ([#366](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/366)).
