- An HTML signature now goes out as it is shown. Its style sheet goes with it, applying only to the signature so it can't restyle the rest of the message or a quoted original. It can have pictures of its own. A draft opened again keeps its signature, table and all, and so does a message taken back with Undo Send, Cancel Send or Open Draft: sent again, it goes with the signature looking as it did and its picture in place, not as a separate attachment. A picture that can't be sent (one naming a file on this computer that wasn't brought in with Import HTML…, or a picture written into the HTML) is refused with the reason instead of being left out of the message ([#1652](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/1652)).
