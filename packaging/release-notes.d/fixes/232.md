- **Google sign-in keeps working with Gmail and account preferences.** Gmail and Google Workspace sign-ins can connect to Google's incoming and outgoing mail servers while tokens remain restricted to their own provider. Changing an unrelated account preference during sign-in no longer discards the completed sign-in; removing the account or changing its credential identity promptly cancels the pending sign-in and clears its waiting status ([#232](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/232)).
- **Connect Calendar and Contacts uses a Microsoft account.** When a Google account is listed first, the command still selects a Microsoft account; explicitly choosing a Google account reports the mismatch before opening sign-in ([#232](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/232)).
