- **Google sign-in keeps working with Gmail and account preferences.** Gmail and Google Workspace sign-ins can connect to Google's incoming and outgoing mail servers while tokens remain restricted to their own provider. Changing an unrelated account preference during sign-in no longer discards the completed sign-in; removing the account or changing its credential identity still rejects the pending response ([#232](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/232)).
