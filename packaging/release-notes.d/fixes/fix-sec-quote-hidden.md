- A reply or forward of an HTML message quotes only what the reading pane shows of it, in HTML and in plain text: however the message nests a template, a hidden block or an element that is left out whole, and as the message looked at the pane's width and in its theme. Text after a template inside an SVG picture or a hidden block is shown again. Outlook's list markers are quoted as the pane shows them; a tooltip, and the alt text of a picture that is shown, are no longer quoted; a message the pane shows as plain text is quoted as that text.
