- Renaming or deleting a category no longer changes it on events in other people's calendars, nor, on a Microsoft account whose categories are kept in step with Outlook, on the mail and other items of its shared mailboxes: those keep the name they had ([#661](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/661)).
- On a Microsoft account added by an alias or a sign-in name, the events in your other calendars now give reminders, and those calendars are listed with your own and can be sent with Email Calendar; before, they were taken for somebody else's and listed under Shared Calendars ([#661](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/661)).
- Renaming or deleting a category now changes it on the events of a calendar kept on a server, there too; before, those events kept the old name on the server ([#661](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/661)).
