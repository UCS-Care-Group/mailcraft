- A calendar or Microsoft To Do update that came back signed in as somebody else than the account is now refused whole, on the desktop and on Android: nothing is sent with that sign-in (no new meeting or to-do goes up to somebody else's account), nothing of it reaches your calendar or your to-dos, its sign-in isn't kept, and you are told to sign in again as the account. Mail kept in step through Microsoft Graph is checked the same way: with somebody else's sign-in no message leaves your Outbox and nothing of your mail is read or changed. Before, the desktop and the phone's To Do took in what it brought and refused only the sign-in ([#1576](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/pull/1576)).
