Each lists the same certificates on every system: a private key that may be used for mail, RSA of 2048 bits or more or P-256/P-384, a certificate without a duplicated or unknown critical extension, expired ones marked (subject and issuer cleaned as every incoming name, addresses, dates, SHA-256 fingerprint); signs a SHA-256, SHA-384 or SHA-512 digest (RSA PKCS#1 v1.5 or PSS, ECDSA P-256 and P-384) only with a certificate for mail that is in its dates, and unwraps an RSA-encrypted message key, an expired certificate's too; a token's key is used only when its public half is the certificate's.
