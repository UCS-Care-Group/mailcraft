| 2026-11-23 | IMAP accounts read every namespace the server names (RFC 2342), not only their own. Folders in another user's or a public namespace are listed in the server's tree under the account. The levels above them that can't be opened, or aren't listed at all (`shared`, `shared/<user>`), are added as folders that only hold others and are never opened. Such folders are never the account's Inbox or a special folder, by flag, by name or by an earlier tie. A mailbox the server lists twice is one folder. Folders an earlier version kept flat at the top ("INBOX (2)") are moved into their place at the next run, with the server's name, never made twice. Their mail is read as any folder's is. |
