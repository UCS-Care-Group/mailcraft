| 2026-11-05 | #580, part 1: a paragraph of a message or a signature is aligned left, centred or right (`format.align {"value": "left"\|"center"\|"right"}`, over the paragraphs the selection or `from` and `to` touch, undone inside the message). It goes out as `text-align` on the paragraph's `<div>`, inline, as Outlook writes it, comes back on a paste and when a draft reopens, and a picture is aligned with its line, the one above a signature's text included. A list item is always left, so a nested list never inherits a centring and every mail program shows the same thing; a blank line's alignment is not kept by HTML. Stored only when it isn't left, so a mailbox written before reads and writes as it did, a details signature still reads as made from its details, and a value a later version writes reads as left without costing a signature its pictures. Clear All Formatting puts it back to left. Bullets, Numbering and Clear All Formatting take the paragraphs a selection takes as alignment does: a line selected with its break no longer makes the line after it a list item, nor takes its bullet or number away. The ribbon's buttons and the keys come in part 2; until then the Commands list doesn't offer it. |
