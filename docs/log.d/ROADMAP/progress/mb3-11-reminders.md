| 2026-11-16 | MB3.11: Android reminders. The core gives the next 64 calendar and task reminders with the instant each is due and a key that holds from one ask to the next (`Core.reminder_schedule`, over the engine's new `view::upcoming_reminders`), and says when they changed (`ReminderScheduleChanged`). The app sets an alarm for each (exact through `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, within the system's window when exact alarms aren't allowed), replaces or calls off those of events that moved or were deleted, and sets them again at every open, after a restart, an update and a change of the clock or the zone. A reminder's notification has its own channel, Dismiss and Snooze, and shows only "Reminders" on a lock screen that hides private content; the Settings switch Reminders calls off every alarm |
