| 2026-11-08 | #893: each account can have a colour. Account Settings › Colour picks one of the twelve colours categories take, or none; a new account is given the first of them no other account has, and an account from before has none until one is chosen (nothing changes in its file until then). The colour is a dot after the account's name in the folder pane, and in a search of All Mailboxes with more than one account, a stripe down the left edge of each row of that account's mail, selected or not, ringed in the text's colour in High Contrast; lists of one folder or one mailbox stay as they were. `account.add` and `account.edit` take `color` (`"#RRGGBB"`, any colour, or `null` for none), and `account.list` says it.
