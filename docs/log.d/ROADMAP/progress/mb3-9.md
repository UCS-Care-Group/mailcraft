| 2026-11-13 | MB3.9: the Android composer. Reply, Reply all, Forward and New message go through the engine's compose commands; the body is the engine's own rich text (bold, italic, underline, lists, links, pictures) drawn natively, with no web view. To, Cc and Bcc suggest from the contacts, From lists the accounts and shared mailboxes, and each account has a signature (Settings) that is swapped when From changes. Attachments come from the document picker, with a safe name, a kind taken from the bytes and caps (25 MB a file, 50 MB in all). A draft is saved by the engine a few seconds after the last edit and when the app leaves the screen. Send queues the message in the phone's mailbox and the screen says sending from the phone is not available yet (the outbox waits for the sync driver, and the mailbox is kept on disk only with MB3.3, so a draft does not survive a restart yet); a `mailto:` link in the reading pane opens the composer filled in, never sent by itself. The binding gains `compose_*`, `suggest_recipients`, `compose_accounts` and `account_signature` |
