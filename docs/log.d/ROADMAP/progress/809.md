| 2026-11-05 | A To Do task's days are the owner's ([#809](https://github.com/UCS-Care-Group/mailcraft/issues/809)): Microsoft Graph gives a task's start and due dates as the owner's midnight turned into UTC (10 November on UTC+1 is `2026-11-09T23:00:00` UTC), and the date was read off the text, so east of UTC every date came a day early. A midnight as written is still its day in the zone it names; any other time is the instant it names, and the day is the one whose midnight is nearest in the zone the calendar is shown in (on either side of a change of the clocks too). A date set here goes as that zone's midnight, named as Graph knows it (`W. Europe Standard Time`; in UTC for a zone Graph has no name for and for a day whose midnight the clocks skip), where it went as midnight UTC, a day early in To Do west of UTC. Tasks already brought down are put right by the next download, and nothing is sent back for them; a date an earlier version sent from west of UTC stays a day early in To Do until it is set again. The zone the calendar is shown in stands for To Do's: when File › Options names a zone other than the one To Do is used in, a date set here can show a day off there. Tests in `task_dates_tests.rs` |
