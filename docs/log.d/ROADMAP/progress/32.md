| 2026-11-12 | #32 part 1: printing. A new crate, `mailcraft-print`, lays a message's display blocks out on pages under its header and writes a PDF with each face embedded as the subset of glyphs used and the system's faces for other scripts behind the bundled ones; `file.print` and `file.saveAsPdf`; the Print dialog sends the job through CUPS (`lpstat`, `lp`, no shell) on macOS, Linux and FreeBSD, and opens the PDF in the viewer elsewhere. Layout, picture, page-break and hostile-input tests, engine and window tests. |
